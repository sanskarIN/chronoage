# Code of Conduct

ChronoAge contributors are expected to create a welcoming, respectful, harassment-free collaboration environment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while still addressing harmful behavior clearly.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Help make technical discussions accessible to contributors with different experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, repeated personal attacks, or disruption of project spaces are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `sanskarin@outlook.in`. Reports should include enough context to evaluate the incident. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action to protect the project community.

This project may update this policy as the contributor community grows.
